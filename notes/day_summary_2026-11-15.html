Headline result — can a blinded reader see KRAS on H&E?

116 colorectal MSS pairs, each a KRAS-mutant slide matched to a KRAS/BRAF wild-type slide from the same hospital. One blinded reader per pair, 8 tumour-epithelium fields per slide, no literature hints given. Separately, all 232 slides were re-coded with random IDs and morphologically graded by eight independent blinded graders.

What this means. There is a reproducible KRAS-associated morphological signal on H&E that is not simply tumour grade, but it is weak and the readers are uncalibrated (all probabilities fell between 0.38 and 0.67; Brier 0.228).

The main confounder. PIK3CA is co-mutated in 34.5% of the KRAS arm vs 15.5% of wild-type, and accuracy was higher in PIK3CA-discordant pairs (72% vs 59%). That is why the next experiment matches on PIK3CA.

Where things stand this morning

Paused, resumable: the embedding run stops and restarts cleanly — a slide is skipped only when both feature files exist, so only the slide in flight at pause time is ever repeated. 846 slides left, roughly $13 and ~2 hours on four containers.

Waiting on a decision: the PIK3CA-matched follow-up is packaged at 54 pairs × 6 fields per reader. Dropping to 4–5 fields would keep 63–83 pairs instead — more pairs, less tissue each.

Waiting on a human: the 30-slide spot-check needs a pathologist before the blinded AI grades can be trusted as a measurement.

Caveats to state out loud

  • All readers and graders are the same model, so their agreement is not independent replication. Inter-reader κ is the point of the 3-reader follow-up.
  • 116 of 145 matched pairs were analysed; 27 were dropped for insufficient sampled tumour. More mutant than wild-type slides fell short, which could bias toward easier mutant cases.
  • Readers saw 8 fields (~2 mm²) per slide, not the whole slide, and most viewed at least some fields downsampled — nuclear-detail scores (IELs, budding, signet rings) are lower-confidence than architecture and mucin.
  • Phikon was pretrained on ~40M TCGA tiles including this cohort's tumour types, so TCGA-internal cross-validation will read optimistically. That is exactly what the CPTAC external set is for: the TCGA-minus-CPTAC gap measures the contamination.
